CASE FILE: Evidence Summary - Cross-Group Coordination

Incident 1 - Location: Central Plaza
James Carter was seen meeting Laura Bennett near Central Plaza. Laura Bennett and Michael Ross were reported together shortly after, exchanging a bag.

Incident 2 - Location: Harbor Street
Michael Ross was later seen meeting Thomas Reed at Harbor Street. Thomas Reed appeared to receive instructions from Michael Ross before leaving separately.

Incident 3 - Location: Market Lane
Thomas Reed was identified at Market Lane in the company of Claire Dawson and Henry Walsh. Claire Dawson and Henry Walsh were seen loading boxes into a van while Thomas Reed supervised.

Incident 4 - Location: Old Mill Road
Thomas Reed was again seen at Old Mill Road, this time meeting Natalie Cross. Natalie Cross was later seen with George Pryce, and George Pryce was seen with Olivia Marsh at the same location on a separate occasion.

Summary:
Thomas Reed is the only individual confirmed present across multiple groups, connecting the Carter-Bennett-Ross group, the Dawson-Walsh group, and the Cross-Pryce-Marsh group. Investigators believe Thomas Reed acts as the central coordinator linking these otherwise separate groups.
