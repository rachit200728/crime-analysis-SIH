CASE FILE: Evidence Summary - Multi-Cell Trafficking Investigation

Witness statements and surveillance summaries collected during the investigation are listed below.

Incident 1 - Cedar Point Warehouse, April 2:
Nathan Price was observed meeting with Victor Hale and Gregory Simmons inside Cedar Point Warehouse. Victor Hale and Gregory Simmons were seen exchanging sealed packages near the loading dock. Nathan Price appeared to supervise the exchange and left separately in an unmarked vehicle.

Incident 2 - Bayview Shipping Yard, April 5:
Nathan Price was later identified at Bayview Shipping Yard in the company of Melissa Grant and Ryan Osborne. Melissa Grant and Ryan Osborne were photographed inspecting shipping containers registered under a false manifest. Nathan Price was again seen coordinating the group from a nearby office.

Incident 3 - Fairview Financial Plaza, April 8:
A separate meeting was recorded at Fairview Financial Plaza involving Melissa Grant and Patricia Nolan. The two discussed wire transfers connected to the shipping yard operation. Patricia Nolan was also seen in the company of Andrew Whitfield, who arrived shortly after the meeting concluded.

Incident 4 - Lakeside Storage Units, April 10:
Nathan Price met again with Gregory Simmons, this time at Lakeside Storage Units, along with two new contacts, Emily Sanders and Brian Coleman. Gregory Simmons, Emily Sanders and Brian Coleman were seen loading crates into a rented truck. Nathan Price observed the loading process before departing.

Incident 5 - Northbridge Industrial Park, April 13:
Andrew Whitfield was seen at Northbridge Industrial Park meeting with Diane Foster and Kevin Marsh. Diane Foster and Kevin Marsh were reported discussing customs paperwork with an unidentified official. Andrew Whitfield left the site before the meeting concluded.

Incident 6 - Eastgate Motel, April 15:
Nathan Price was recorded briefly meeting Diane Foster at Eastgate Motel. No other individuals were confirmed present at this meeting, though investigators believe this contact links the financial group to the industrial park group.

Incident 7 - Riverside Cargo Terminal, April 18:
Ryan Osborne and Brian Coleman were seen together at Riverside Cargo Terminal, along with a new individual, Samuel Kirk. Samuel Kirk was observed transferring documents to Ryan Osborne before leaving the terminal separately.

Summary:
Across all seven incidents, Nathan Price is the individual confirmed present at the widest range of locations, appearing to link the warehouse group (Victor Hale, Gregory Simmons), the shipping yard group (Melissa Grant, Ryan Osborne), the storage unit group (Emily Sanders, Brian Coleman), and, through his direct contact with Diane Foster, the financial and industrial groups as well (Patricia Nolan, Andrew Whitfield, Kevin Marsh, Samuel Kirk). Investigators believe Nathan Price is functioning as the central coordinator connecting otherwise separate cells, while individuals such as Patricia Nolan, Kevin Marsh and Samuel Kirk remain confined to their local groups with no direct connection to the others.
