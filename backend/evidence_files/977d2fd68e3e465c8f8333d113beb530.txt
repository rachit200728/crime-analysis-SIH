Investigation Report - Case CL-2024-007

On the night of the incident, Arjun Mehta was seen coordinating with Sameer Khan near Connaught Place, Delhi. Both individuals are believed to have connections with Farhan Ali, who allegedly supplied materials through a local contact named Rizwan Ahmed.

Surveillance footage from Karol Bagh shows Priya Nair meeting with Arjun Mehta two days prior to the incident. Investigators suspect she may have facilitated communication between the group members.

A sixth individual, Deepak Chauhan, was flagged after his vehicle was spotted near the same location during the same timeframe. His connection to Rizwan Ahmed is currently under review.

The group is suspected to be linked to a network operating under the name Crescent Trading Company, registered in an undisclosed location.
