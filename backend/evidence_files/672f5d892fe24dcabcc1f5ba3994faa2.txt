CASE FILE: Evidence Summary - Warehouse Fraud Investigation

Witness statements collected during the investigation are summarized below.

Incident 1 - Riverside Docks, March 12:
Daniel Cole was seen meeting with Marcus Webb near the loading bay at Riverside Docks. Marcus Webb and Oliver Grant were reported exchanging a black duffel bag inside the warehouse office. Surveillance footage also placed Daniel Cole at the same location earlier that evening, coordinating with both men.

Incident 2 - Northgate Storage Facility, March 15:
Daniel Cole was later identified at Northgate Storage Facility, this time in the company of Sophie Turner and Ethan Brooks. Sophie Turner and Ethan Brooks were seen loading unmarked crates into a white van registered to a shell company. Daniel Cole appeared to be directing the operation from a nearby vehicle.

Incident 3 - Harbor View Apartments, March 18:
A separate meeting was recorded at Harbor View Apartments involving Sophie Turner and Isabella Reyes. The two discussed financial transfers linked to the storage facility operation. No other persons were confirmed present at this meeting.

Incident 4 - Eastside Freight Yard, March 20:
Daniel Cole met again with Oliver Grant, this time at Eastside Freight Yard, along with a new contact named Lucas Hart. Oliver Grant and Lucas Hart were seen inspecting freight containers, while Daniel Cole observed from a distance and made several phone calls.

Summary:
Across all four incidents, Daniel Cole is the only individual confirmed present at every location, appearing to link the docks group (Marcus Webb, Oliver Grant), the storage group (Sophie Turner, Ethan Brooks), and the freight yard group (Oliver Grant, Lucas Hart). Investigators believe Daniel Cole may be functioning as a central coordinator between otherwise separate cells. Isabella Reyes appears connected only through Sophie Turner and has no direct link to the other groups.
