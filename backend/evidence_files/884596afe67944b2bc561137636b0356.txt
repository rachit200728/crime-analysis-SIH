The November 10, 2025, Delhi bomb blast was a high-intensity suicide car explosion near the historic Red Fort that killed 14 people and injured dozens of others.


Incident Overview

Date and Time: November 10, 2025, at approximately 6:52 p.m.

Location: Near a busy traffic signal and Gate 1 of the Red Fort Metro Station in New Delhi.

Casualties: 14 people were killed (including the suicide bomber) and over 30 others sustained injuries.

Nature of Attack: A white Hyundai i20 packed with explosives detonated at a red light, turning the vehicle into a fireball and damaging surrounding traffic.

Prime Accused: Dr. Umar-un-Nabi, a doctor and assistant professor at Al Falah University in Faridabad, acted as the suicide bomber and died in the explosion
